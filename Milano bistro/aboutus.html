The Milano Bistro story

Rooted in tradition.
Made for today.

Milano Bistro was imagined as a celebration of Italian dining: honest ingredients, generous flavours and the simple pleasure of sharing food. From the first pour of olive oil to the final spoonful of dessert, every detail is considered.

Our menu takes inspiration from familiar Italian classics while giving each dish a fresh, contemporary presentation. We want every visit to feel relaxed, memorable and a little special.
